01 — About

Who
I Am.

I'm a Undergraduate student studying in Polaris school of technology Bangalore from Delhi with a knack for building things — whether that's a Python health tracker, an electromagnetic induction model, or shooting with a full-frame camera. Every build carries weight and significance.

My interests live at the crossroads of hardware and software. I've done Arduino projects, studied ECE-level circuits, and built software from the ground up. I think in systems — how things connect, why they work, and how to make them better.

Outside of tech, I'm into cars, Formula 1, PC hardware, photography, and gaming. The same obsessive attention to detail applies to all of it. Physics simulations, graphics, and ease of control and usage along with a generous learning curve
